HOW TO USE THIS
---------------
A CSV version of this file (KEYWORD-RESEARCH-SHEET.csv) has columns for
section, group, keyword, volume, kd, intent and notes — paste the keyword
column straight into your keyword tool, then fill in the numbers.

578 keywords, ZERO of which the site currently targets (verified against
lib/seo-config.ts), so there is no cannibalisation risk.

HOW TO PRIORITISE
------------------
1. Fill in VOLUME and KD for every keyword.
2. Sort by VOLUME desc, then KD asc.
3. Apply the RULES below to decide what to build.

BUILD RULES (learned from the last keyword round)
-------------------------------------------------
* KD 0-8 + volume >1,000  -> BUILD A NEW PAGE. Low competition, real demand.
* KD 9-20 + volume >5,000 -> BUILD A PAGE or a DEEP-DIVE SECTION.
* KD 9-20 + volume <5,000 -> FAQ ANSWER on the existing page only.
* KD 21+  -> DO NOT TARGET. Not worth the effort for a new site.
* Any keyword with a government/exam brand in it (UPSC, SSC, NEET, IBPS...)
  is usually LOW KD even at high volume, because the big sites publish
  generic content that does not match the exact spec query.

CONTENT QUALITY RULE (non-negotiable)
--------------------------------------
Never ship a page that is only a reworded version of another page.
Google's "scaled content abuse" policy targets pages generated at scale
that target near-identical queries. Every new page needs:
  - its own genuinely different tool or calculation,
  - at least 1,500 words of ORIGINAL content with unique tables/figures,
  - 8+ unique FAQs that the other page does not answer,
  - its own canonical URL and its own internal links.

If a keyword only needs different wording, add it as an FAQ answer or an
H2/H3 on the EXISTING page (see PART 2) instead of creating a page.
