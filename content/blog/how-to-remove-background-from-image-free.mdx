## Pro Tips: Handling Flyaway Hair, Jewelry & Glass Reflections

Intricate edges like curly hair, semi-transparent glassware, and delicate jewelry are notoriously challenging for automated cutouts. Follow these three pro strategies to get studio-grade results every time:

1. **Maximize Subject-to-Background Contrast:** When shooting photos, avoid wearing a white shirt against a white wall. The greater the color contrast between foreground and background, the crisper the edge segmentation.
2. **Crop Away Unnecessary Elements First:** If your photo has clutter in the corners (like lamps, furniture, or passing pedestrians), use our [Image Cropper Tool](/image/crop) before removing the background. This concentrates the neural attention on the main subject.
3. **Format for Product Listings:** Once you isolate your product on a white background, use our [Image Resizer](/image/resize) to standardize your catalog to 2000 x 2000 pixels (1:1 square ratio) for optimal Amazon zoom functionality.
