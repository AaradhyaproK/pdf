The National Testing Agency (NTA) mandates two distinct photographs for candidates appearing in the **NEET UG (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test)** medical examination:

1. **Passport Size Photograph** (for application upload and admit card generation)
2. **Postcard Size Photograph (4" × 6")** (to be pasted on the proforma document on exam day)

Uploading improper photographs or missing candidate name and date stamps can result in cancellation of candidature. Here are the official NTA guidelines.

---

> **Official Portal Verification Notice:** Photo guidelines are set by the National Testing Agency. Always review the latest NEET Information Bulletin on `neet.nta.nic.in` before submitting.

---

## 1. NEET UG Passport Size Photograph Specifications

- **File Weight:** **10 KB to 200 KB**.
- **Dimensions:** Standard passport size (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm).
- **Background:** White background only.
- **Face Coverage:** **80% facial coverage** with both ears clearly visible.
- **Name & Date of Photo:** Candidate's name and the date of taking the photograph must be printed at the bottom of the photo.
- **Spectacles:** Permitted only if used regularly; no tinted lenses or flash reflection allowed.

---

## 2. NEET UG Postcard Size Photograph Specifications (4" × 6")

- **Physical Dimensions:** **4 inches × 6 inches** (Aspect ratio 2:3 or 4:6).
- **Digital Resolution:** At 300 DPI, approximately **1200 × 1800 pixels**.
- **File Weight:** **50 KB to 300 KB**.
- **Image Continuity:** The postcard photograph must be the **exact same photograph** taken on the same date as the passport-size photo.

---

## 3. NEET Signature & Fingerprint Requirements

- **Signature File Weight:** **4 KB to 30 KB** in JPG format on white paper.
- **Left and Right Hand Fingers and Thumb Impressions:** **10 KB to 200 KB** on white paper using blue ink.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Different Photos for Passport & Postcard:** NTA guidelines explicitly mandate that the passport photo and postcard photo must be identical captures.
- **Omitting Name and Date:** Failing to include candidate name and date stamp on either photo violates application instructions.
- **Dark or Patterned Backgrounds:** Only clean white backgrounds are accepted.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Do I need the same photo for both Passport and Postcard sizes in NEET?
Yes. The National Testing Agency requires that the passport-size and postcard-size photographs be printed from the same original portrait session.

### What should be written on the NEET photograph stamp?
The candidate's full name (as recorded in the Class 10 certificate) and the date on which the photo was taken (e.g. `RAHUL SHARMA | 15.08.2026`).

### What format must NEET photos be uploaded in?
All photographs, signatures, and impressions must be uploaded in standard **JPG / JPEG** format.
