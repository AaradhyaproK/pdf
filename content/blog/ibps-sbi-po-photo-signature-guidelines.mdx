## Exact Wording for IBPS & SBI Handwritten Declaration (2026-2027)

The handwritten declaration is one of the most strictly audited uploads in banking forms. It must be written by the candidate in their own handwriting on clean white A4 paper using a **black ink pen** only. Writing in blue ink or typing the text is strictly disallowed.

### Official Declaration Text:

> *"I, \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (Name of the candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required."*

### Crucial Declaration Rules:
1. **Language:** Must be written in **English only**. Declarations written in Hindi or regional languages are treated as invalid.
2. **Capital Letters Prohibited:** The declaration must **NOT be written in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS**. Use natural sentence case (capitalizing only the letter "I" and the first letter of your name).
3. **No Third-Party Writing:** If at any stage during Document Verification (DV) or joining, the handwriting does not match your handwriting sample collected during the preliminary and mains exams, your candidature will be cancelled and criminal proceedings may be initiated.

## Left Thumb Impression (LTI): Guidelines & Exceptions

Banking exams require biometric verification during the exam hall entry and interview stages. Your uploaded digital thumb impression serves as the baseline biometric imprint:

* **Stamp Pad:** Use a clean, freshly inked blue or black ink stamp pad. Avoid excess ink that smudges the ridge patterns.
* **Paper:** Use crisp white unlined bond paper.
* **Scan Clarity:** Ensure the fingerprint lines (whorls and loops) are clearly distinguishable without blur.

### What if the Left Thumb is Injured or Missing?
Official IBPS and SBI notifications provide clear accommodations if a candidate does not have a left thumb:
1. **Right Thumb:** You may use your Right Thumb impression.
2. **Other Fingers:** If both thumbs are unavailable, use the index finger of the left hand, moving through the remaining fingers.
3. **No Fingers:** If no fingers are available, the impression of the left toe may be uploaded.
4. **Mandatory Declaration:** In all such exception cases, mention clearly on the document sheet which digit's impression has been affixed (e.g., *"Right Thumb Impression affixed due to injury on left hand"*).

## Top 5 Reasons IBPS & SBI Photos Get Rejected (Avoid These Mistakes!)

1. **Selfies or Casual Mobile Photos:** Selfies taken at dynamic angles or with filtered portrait blur are instantly detected and flagged. Only front-facing, neutral-expression studio photos are accepted.
2. **Dark Sunglasses & Flash Reflections on Spectacles:** If you wear prescription glasses, ensure there is zero flash glare obscuring your pupils. Tinted glasses and sunglasses are completely barred.
3. **Caps, Hats, or Heavy Shadows:** Religious head coverings (such as turbans or hijabs) are permitted, provided the full face from the bottom of the chin to the top of the forehead and both cheeks remain completely visible.
4. **File Size Below Minimum Floor:** Uploading a photo of 18 KB when the requirement is 20 KB–50 KB will trigger an automated upload error on the portal: *"Specified file size is less than 20 KB"*.
5. **Old or Unrecognizable Photograph:** The photograph must have been captured within the last **3 months**. Uploading school-era or decade-old photos can cause detention at the biometric frisking gate on exam day.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I use blue ink for the IBPS signature?
No. Official IBPS and SBI notifications explicitly state that signatures must be done in **black ink pen** only on white paper. Blue ink signatures can result in form rejection or portal validation flags.

### What is the exact size required for SBI PO photograph and signature?
For SBI PO 2026-2027, the photograph must be between **20 KB and 50 KB** (dimensions: 200 x 230 pixels), and the signature must be between **10 KB and 20 KB** (dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels) in JPG/JPEG format.

### Is the handwritten declaration required for both IBPS PO and IBPS Clerk?
Yes. Both IBPS PO (Probationary Officers) and IBPS Clerk (Customer Service Associates), as well as IBPS RRB Officer Scale I and Office Assistant, mandate uploading a handwritten declaration between 50 KB and 100 KB.

### Can I write the IBPS declaration in capital letters?
No. The declaration must NOT be written in capital letters. It must be written in normal cursive or sentence-case handwriting. Declarations in capital letters will be rejected.

### Can I take a photo of my signature from a smartphone?
Yes, you can snap a clean, well-lit photo of your signature using a smartphone. However, make sure there are no shadows on the paper, crop out the extra borders, and run it through our [IBPS Photo Resizer](/tools/ibps-photo-resizer) to convert it to exact 140x60 pixels and 10-20 KB.
