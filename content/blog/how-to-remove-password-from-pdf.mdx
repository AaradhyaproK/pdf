## User Password vs. Owner Password: What Is the Difference?

The official PDF specification (**ISO 32000-1**) defines two distinct types of security locks:

| Password Type | What It Restricts | How FileZenith Unlocks It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Document Open Password (User Password)** | Prevents anyone from viewing or opening the document without entering the decryption key. | You enter the password once to prove ownership. The engine decodes the 128-bit or 256-bit AES cipher in memory and saves a clean unencrypted copy. |
| **Permissions Password (Owner Password)** | Allows opening the file, but restricts printing, copying text, adding annotations, or extracting pages. | FileZenith instantly strips the restriction flags from the PDF trailer dictionary without requiring any password! |

## Common Default Passwords for Indian Bank Statements & e-Aadhaar

If you have forgotten the password scheme for an official document, here are the most common standard formats used across major institutions:

- **e-Aadhaar Card:** First 4 letters of your legal name in CAPITAL letters + your 4-digit Year of Birth (e.g., if name is RAHUL and birth year is 1995: `RAHU1995`).
- **SBI Bank Statement:** 11-digit account number, or 5-digit registered mobile number + 4-digit DOB (`DDMM`).
- **HDFC Bank Statement:** Your 9-digit Customer ID, or first 4 letters of name + DDMM of birth.
- **ICICI Bank Statement:** 4-digit date & month of birth (`DDMM`) + last 4 digits of account number.
- **EPFO / PF Passbook:** Your 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN) or PAN card number in uppercase.
