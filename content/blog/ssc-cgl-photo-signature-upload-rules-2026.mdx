## 🚫 Top 6 Reasons SSC Rejects Photos and Signatures

Understanding why automated filters reject uploads helps you avoid costly errors:

### 1. Wearing Spectacles, Glasses, or Sunglasses
SSC has enforced a strict zero-tolerance policy on eyeglasses. Even clear prescription reading glasses frequently cause lens glare, flash reflections, or partially obscure the pupil and iris. **Always remove spectacles before capturing your live photo.**

### 2. File Size Outside the Permitted Range
* If your photograph is **19.8 KB**, the portal will block upload because it falls below the minimum **20 KB** requirement.
* If your signature is **20.4 KB**, the portal will immediately trigger an error.
* Using [FileZenith's SSC Photo Resizer](/tools/ssc-photo-resizer) automatically locks your output between **20 KB and 50 KB**.

### 3. Wearing Caps, Hats, or Head Coverings
Hats, bandanas, scarves, and beanies are strictly prohibited. While religious headwear is permitted, your entire face from the bottom of your chin to the top of your forehead and both ears must remain clearly visible.

### 4. Direct Selfie Distortion & Uneven Shadows
Taking a casual selfie by holding your phone close to your face distorts your facial proportions (known as fisheye distortion). Dark shadows behind your head or under your chin trigger automated contrast rejections. Stand facing a bright window or evenly lit light source.

### 5. Signature on Lined or Ruled Notebook Paper
SSC requires signatures to be made on **plain white unruled paper**. Signatures written on lined, squared, or textured notebook pages will be flagged by automated scanners.

### 6. Signatures in ALL CAPITAL Letters
Signing in full capital letters (e.g., `RAHUL SHARMA`) is invalid according to official commission rules. Your signature must be in your natural running cursive handwriting.

## 📸 How to Ace the SSC Live Web Camera Capture on ssc.gov.in

Under the One-Time Registration (OTR) module, candidates must capture a live photo using their smartphone camera or computer webcam. Here is the step-by-step checklist to pass:

1. **Find a Plain White Wall:** Stand 2 to 3 feet in front of a clean, plain white or light-coloured wall. Avoid textured tiles, curtains, or cluttered rooms.
2. **Face the Light Source:** Natural daylight facing a window is best. Never have a light bulb, tube light, or window directly behind your head.
3. **Keep Your Eyes Level with the Lens:** Keep your shoulders straight, look directly into the camera lens with a neutral expression (no wide grins), and keep both ears visible.
4. **Remove Headphone & Earphones:** Ensure no wireless earbuds or wires are visible.
