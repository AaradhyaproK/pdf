## The 5 Fatal Mistakes That Cause RRB Application Rejection

The Railway Recruitment Board deploys automated optical validation software paired with human scrutiny boards. Applications containing any of the following flaws are disqualified with **zero refund and no correction opportunity**:

### 1. Wearing Spectacles, Sunglasses, or Colored Contact Lenses
Even if you wear prescription power glasses daily, RRB rules explicitly mandate that the photograph must be captured **without spectacles or tinted glasses**. Glare or optical reflection on spectacle lenses obscures pupil coordinates, preventing automated facial biometric matching on exam day.

### 2. Colored, Gradient, or Dark Backgrounds
Selfies captured against bedroom walls, outdoor garden backgrounds, or curtains with floral prints are flagged automatically. The backdrop must be **pure white or solid off-white**. If your original photo has a cluttered background, use FileZenith's [Background Remover](/image/remove-background) to instantly replace it with a studio-grade white backdrop.

### 3. Signatures in Block / CAPITAL Letters
Signing as `RAHUL KUMAR` instead of natural running cursive handwriting results in instant rejection. Signatures in capital letters are legally unenforceable and deemed invalid by Indian Railways. Sign naturally on clean, unlined white paper with a bold black ballpoint pen.

### 4. Re-using Old Photographs from Past Exam Cycles
Railway notifications stipulate that the photograph must have been captured **not more than three months prior** to the notification release date. The facial appearance in the uploaded photo must match your physical appearance on exam day (hair length, facial hair, etc.) to clear biometric frisking at the TCS iON exam center.

### 5. Blurry or Over-Compressed SC/ST Travel Pass PDFs
Reserved category applicants entitled to free sleeper rail travel must upload their Central/State Caste Certificate in PDF format. If compression renders the issuing authority's round stamp or digital seal illegible, the free travel request is cancelled and the admit card may be withheld. ## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — RRB NTPC & Group D

### 1. What are the exact photo size limits for RRB NTPC and Group D?
The photograph must be in JPG or JPEG format, with a file size strictly between **30 KB and 70 KB**. The physical dimensions must be **35 mm width by 45 mm height** (or roughly 320 x 240 pixels), captured on a clean, solid white background.

### 2. Can I wear spectacles in my RRB application photograph?
No. Official Railway Recruitment Board guidelines strictly prohibit wearing spectacles, sunglasses, or tinted glasses in the application photo. Even if you wear prescription glasses, you must remove them for the photo to ensure clear optical biometric matching.

### 3. What happens if my photo size is 25 KB or 75 KB?
The `rrbapply.gov.in` upload filter will immediately reject your file. Files under 30.0 KB or over 70.0 KB fail the server pre-check validation rules. Use FileZenith's compressor targeting 45 KB to 55 KB for guaranteed acceptance.

### 4. Is Date of Photo (DOP) or Candidate Name compulsory on RRB photos?
Unlike some SSC notifications, Indian Railways does not strictly mandate printing your name or date on the photo unless explicitly stated in an addendum. However, the photo must be recent (taken within the last 3 months), and adding a subtle date stamp does not disqualify your application.

### 5. What are the signature dimensions and ink color for Railway forms?
The signature must be signed on unlined white paper with black or dark blue ink. Dimensions must be **50 mm x 20 mm** (or approximately 140 x 60 pixels), and the file size must be strictly between **15 KB and 30 KB**. Capital letter signatures are strictly rejected.

### 6. Why does my signature get rejected even though it is under 30 KB?
Common reasons include: signing in capital letters, having dark shadows or gray paper backgrounds, using a light pencil or felt pen that bleeds, or uploading an image smaller than 15 KB. Use FileZenith's Magic B&W cleanup to ensure high-contrast black strokes on pure white.

### 7. How do I get a free sleeper class train ticket for RRB exams?
SC and ST candidates must upload a legible copy of their official caste certificate in PDF format under 500 KB during online application submission. If approved, the e-admit card will contain a free railway travel pass authority voucher.
