## 🚚 What is an E-Way Bill (E Way Bill)?

An **e-way bill** (electronic way bill) is a digital document generated on the national GST portal evidencing the physical movement of goods from one location to another. It contains crucial details including:

1. **Consignor (Supplier) Details:** Name, GSTIN, and dispatch dispatch address.
2. **Consignee (Recipient) Details:** Name, GSTIN, and delivery destination address.
3. **Goods Description:** Harmonized System of Nomenclature (HSN) codes, quantity, and taxable value.
4. **Transport Details:** Transporter name, Transporter ID (TRANSIN), vehicle registration number, and Lorry Receipt (LR) / Railway Receipt (RR) / Airway Bill (AWB) number.

Once generated, the GST portal issues a unique **12-digit E-Way Bill Number (EBN)** with a scannable QR code. Tax officers, GST flying squads, and border check-posts verify consignments in real time by scanning this barcode or querying the 12-digit EBN.

## 🔑 E-Way Bill Login: Step-by-Step Portal Access on ewaybillgst.gov.in

To generate or manage e-way bills, access the official unified portal administered by the National Informatics Centre (NIC):

👉 **Official Portal:** [https://ewaybillgst.gov.in](https://ewaybillgst.gov.in)

### Step 1: Open the Official E-Way Bill System Portal
Navigate to `ewaybillgst.gov.in` on your browser. Never use third-party imitation websites to enter your sensitive GST credentials.

### Step 2: Click on the "Login" Button
On the top right corner of the homepage, click the green **"Login"** button to open the authentication screen.

### Step 3: Enter Your Credentials
* **Username:** Enter your registered E-Way Bill portal username (this can be different from or identical to your GST common portal username depending on how you registered).
* **Password:** Enter your secure password.
* **Captcha Code:** Enter the 6-character alphanumeric security code displayed on screen.

### Step 4: Complete Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
If 2FA is activated for your GSTIN, enter the One-Time Password (OTP) received on your registered mobile number or verify via the **NIC-GST-Shield** mobile authenticator app.

### Step 5: Access the E-Way Bill Dashboard
Once logged in, you will land on the main navigation console featuring options for **Generate New**, **Generate Bulk**, **Update Part-B / Vehicle**, **Extend Validity**, and **Print EWB**.

> [!TIP]
> **Forgot E-Way Bill Password or Username?**
> On the login page, click **"Forgot Username"** or **"Forgot Password"**. Enter your 15-digit GSTIN, registered mobile number, and captcha. An OTP will be sent to reset your credentials within 2 minutes.

## 📊 Who Must Generate an E-Way Bill? Mandatory Threshold Limits

Under GST law, an **eway bill** must be generated before commencing the movement of goods in the following circumstances:

| Movement Type | Statutory Value Threshold | Who is Responsible? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inter-State Movement** (Between two different States / UTs) | **Consignment Value > ₹50,000** | Registered supplier or transporter |
| **Intra-State Movement** (Within the same State) | **₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000** (Varies by State) | Registered supplier or recipient |
| **Job Work Movement** (Inter-State) | **Mandatory for ANY value** (Even < ₹50,000) | Principal manufacturer or registered job worker |
| **Handicraft Goods Transportation** | **Mandatory for ANY value** (Inter-State) | Person exempt from GST registration |
| **E-Commerce Deliveries** | **Consignment Value > ₹50,000** | E-commerce operator or courier agency |

### State-Wise Intra-State E-Way Bill Thresholds (2026 Update)
While inter-state movement has a uniform **₹50,000** limit across all of India, individual states have relaxed intra-state limits to ease compliance for small local traders:

* **Maharashtra:** ₹1,00,000 (Relaxed from ₹50,000 for intra-state movement).
* **Delhi:** ₹1,00,000 (No e-way bill required within Delhi NCT for consignments up to ₹1 Lakh).
* **Uttar Pradesh (UP):** ₹50,000 general goods; ₹1,00,000 for select commodities.
* **Gujarat:** ₹50,000 for taxable items; exemptions for local intra-city transit.
* **Bihar:** ₹1,00,000 for intra-state non-sensitive goods.
* **Tamil Nadu & Karnataka:** ₹50,000 standard intra-state limit.

## 📑 Part A vs Part B of E-Way Bill Explained

An **e way bill** is divided into two distinct sections. Understanding both is critical to avoid heavy penalties (up to 200% of tax amount under Section 129 of the CGST Act):

### Part A (Consignment Information):
Filled by the **supplier or recipient**. It locks the transaction data:
* **GSTIN of Supplier & Recipient:** Valid 15-digit GST numbers.
* **Place of Dispatch & Delivery:** Pincodes of origin and destination (used by the portal to calculate valid route distance automatically).
* **Document Number & Date:** Tax Invoice, Bill of Supply, or Delivery Challan number.
* **Value of Goods:** Taxable value + CGST, SGST, IGST, and Cess.
* **HSN Code:** Minimum 4 to 6 digits based on turnover.
* **Reason for Transportation:** Supply, Export/Import, Job Work, SKD/CKD, Line Sale, or Exhibition.

### Part B (Transport & Vehicle Details):
Filled by the **transporter or the supplier** using their own vehicle:
* **Vehicle Registration Number:** e.g., `MH12AB1234` or `DL01XY9876` (for road transit).
* **Transporter Document Number:** Lorry Receipt (LR) number, Railway Receipt (RR) number, Airway Bill (AWB) number, or Bill of Lading (for multi-modal transport).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Generating Part A alone does NOT make an E-Way Bill legally valid for transit!**
> If goods are in physical transit on a truck, **both Part A and Part B must be completed**. Moving goods with only Part A filled is treated as transporting goods without an e-way bill, leading to truck detention and penalties under GST Rule 138.

## 📝 Step-by-Step Guide to Generate E-Way Bill Online

Follow these exact steps once you are authenticated via **eway bill login**:

1. **Navigate to E-Waybill Menu:** On the left sidebar, click **"E-Waybill"** ➔ **"Generate New"**.
2. **Select Transaction Type:**
   * Choose **Outward** (if you are selling or sending goods).
   * Choose **Inward** (if you are receiving goods from an unregistered supplier).
3. **Select Sub-Type:** Select **Supply**, **Export**, **Job Work**, or **For Own Use**.
4. **Enter Document Details:** Select **Tax Invoice**, enter the Invoice Number and Invoice Date.
5. **Enter Consignor & Consignee Details:** If you enter the buyer's GSTIN, their trade name, address, and state auto-populate from the GST database.
6. **Enter Item Information:** Enter product description, HSN code, quantity, unit, taxable amount, and applicable GST rate (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%). Calculate exact tax splits in advance using FileZenith's [GST Calculator](/utility/gst-calculator).
7. **Enter Transporter Details & Part B:**
   * Enter the **Transporter ID** (if handing over to a transport company).
   * OR enter the **Vehicle Number** (if shipping directly via your own transport).
8. **Click Submit:** Review all fields carefully and click **"Submit"**. The portal generates your unique **12-digit E-Way Bill Number (EBN)**.

## ⏱️ E-Way Bill Validity Rules: Distance Calculation & Extension

The validity of an **e-way bill** is strictly time-bound based on the total road distance between the dispatch origin and delivery destination:

| Cargo Type | Distance Calculation | Validity Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regular Cargo (Normal Commercial Vehicles)** | **Up to 200 km** | **1 Day** (24 hours) |
| **Regular Cargo (Additional Distance)** | **For every additional 200 km** (or part thereof) | **+1 Additional Day** |
| **Over Dimensional Cargo (ODC) / Multi-Modal Shipments** | **Up to 20 km** | **1 Day** |
| **Over Dimensional Cargo (Additional Distance)** | **For every additional 20 km** (or part thereof) | **+1 Additional Day** |

### How to Calculate Validity Window:
* **Example:** A consignment moving from Mumbai to New Delhi (~1,450 km):
  * First 200 km = 1 day.
  * Remaining 1,250 km = 1,250 / 200 = 6.25 ➔ 7 additional days.
  * **Total Validity = 8 Days**.
* **Expiry Time:** An e-way bill expires at midnight (12:00 AM) of the final valid date.

### How to Extend E-Way Bill Validity Before Expiry:
If a truck breaks down, faces a traffic blockade, or experiences transshipment delays:
1. Log in to `ewaybillgst.gov.in`.
2. Click **"E-Waybill"** ➔ **"Extend Validity"**.
3. Enter the 12-digit E-Way Bill Number.
4. Validity extension can only be requested **within 8 hours before expiry or within 8 hours after expiry**.
5. Update the new vehicle number, reason for delay, and remaining distance.

## 🖨️ How to Print E-Way Bill in 2-in-1 Format & Save as PDF

After generating an e-way bill, truck drivers must carry either a physical printed copy or a digital PDF on their mobile devices:

1. **Access the Print Screen:** On the portal dashboard, select **"E-Waybill"** ➔ **"Print EWB"**.
2. **Enter EWB Number:** Input the 12-digit number and click **"Go"**.
3. **Choose Print Layout:**
   * **Detailed Print:** Full A4 page showing all line-item HSN codes, invoice breakup, and transporter history.
   * **Print (Standard):** Compact single-sheet format with official QR code.
4. **Save as PDF:** Press `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P` on Mac), change the destination printer to **"Save as PDF"**, and save the file to your computer.

## ❌ How to Cancel or Modify an E-Way Bill

* **Cancellation Window:** An e-way bill can only be cancelled **within 24 hours of generation**.
* **Condition:** It cannot be cancelled if it has already been verified in transit by a GST officer.
* **Steps to Cancel:**
  1. Log in to `ewaybillgst.gov.in`.
  2. Select **"E-Waybill"** ➔ **"Cancel"**.
  3. Enter the 12-digit E-Way Bill number.
  4. Select the cancellation reason: *Order Cancelled*, *Data Entry Mistake*, or *Duplicate Generation*.
  5. Click **Submit**.

> [!WARNING]
> An e-way bill **cannot be edited** once submitted. If you made an error in the taxable value, GST rate, or consignee GSTIN, you **must cancel the e-way bill within 24 hours** and generate a fresh one with the correct invoice details.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the official website for E-Way Bill login?
The only official portal for E-Way Bill generation and management across all Indian states is [https://ewaybillgst.gov.in](https://ewaybillgst.gov.in), developed by the National Informatics Centre (NIC).

### Is an E-Way Bill mandatory for goods below ₹50,000?
No, for general commercial shipments, an e-way bill is not required if the consignment value is ₹50,000 or less. However, it is mandatory regardless of value for inter-state transportation of goods for job work and handicraft goods transported by exempt dealers.

### What is the difference between Part A and Part B of an e-way bill?
Part A contains consignment details including supplier and recipient GSTINs, dispatch address, invoice number, value of goods, and HSN codes. Part B contains transportation information including the vehicle registration number or transporter receipt number (LR/RR/AWB). Both parts must be completed for an e-way bill to be legally valid during physical transit.

### How long is an e-way bill valid?
For regular cargo, validity is 1 day for distances up to 200 km, plus 1 additional day for every subsequent 200 km (or part thereof). For Over Dimensional Cargo (ODC), validity is 1 day for distances up to 20 km, plus 1 additional day for every subsequent 20 km.

### Can I extend the validity of an e-way bill after it expires?
Yes. You can extend an e-way bill validity within 8 hours before its expiration or within 8 hours after its expiration on the official portal under the "Extend Validity" menu.

### Can an e-way bill be edited after generation?
No. An e-way bill cannot be edited once generated. If there is a mistake in invoice value, recipient GSTIN, or HSN, you must cancel it within 24 hours and generate a new e-way bill. However, you can update Part B (vehicle number and transporter) at any time during transit.
