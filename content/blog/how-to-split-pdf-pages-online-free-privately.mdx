## ✂️ 3 Ways to Split a PDF Document

Depending on what you want to achieve, there are three primary methods to separate PDF pages:

### 1. Extract Specific Page Ranges (e.g., Pages 3 to 7)
Best when you have a long report, textbook, or multi-year financial record and only need a specific chapter, quarterly statement, or section. You input `3-7` or select the pages visually, and the tool outputs a single clean PDF containing only those 5 pages.

### 2. Split Every Page into Individual 1-Page PDFs
Ideal when dealing with bulk invoices, student grade sheets, or scanned certificates that need to be filed or emailed individually. Every page is converted into its own dedicated PDF file.

### 3. Extract Non-Consecutive Pages (e.g., Pages 1, 5, 8, 12)
Perfect when preparing a customized application dossier that requires selecting non-adjacent pages from various sections of a larger portfolio.
