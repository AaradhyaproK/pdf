## Official Aadhaar Card Dimensions: The ISO/IEC 7810 Standard

The physical card portion of an official Aadhaar document strictly adheres to the international **ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 standard** (also known as the **CR80 standard**). This is the exact same standardized physical footprint used worldwide for credit cards, debit cards, and national driving licenses.

If you are setting up your photo printer or cutting laminated paper, here is the universal dimension specification matrix:

| Unit of Measurement | Card Width | Card Height | Corner Radius | Recommended Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Millimeters (mm)** | **85.60 mm** | **53.98 mm** | 3.18 mm | 300 DPI minimum |
| **Centimeters (cm)** | **8.56 cm** | **5.40 cm** | 0.32 cm | 300 DPI minimum |
| **Inches (in)** | **3.370 inches** | **2.125 inches** | 0.125 inches | 300 DPI minimum |
| **Pixels (@ 300 DPI)** | **1011 pixels** | **638 pixels** | 38 pixels | High-Definition Printing |
| **Pixels (@ 600 DPI)** | **2022 pixels** | **1276 pixels** | 76 pixels | Commercial Thermal Dye Sublimation |

> **Crucial Rule:** The width-to-height aspect ratio is approximately **1.586 : 1**. Never crop or scale the front and back portions independently using arbitrary drag handles, as doing so alters facial proportions and invalidates document security inspection.

## 5 Critical Printing Pitfalls That Cause Verification Rejections

1. **Ignoring the Aspect Ratio (The "Stretched Face" Error):** Many cyber cafés paste card images into Word documents and pull corner handles, distorting facial geometry. Exam invigilators will reject distorted photo ID proof immediately.
2. **Low-Resolution QR Code Printing:** UIDAI's tamper-proof QR code contains encrypted demographic data and biometric hashes. If printed below 300 DPI, handheld QR barcode scanners used by airline CISF guards and exam centers will fail to decode the code.
3. **Black and White Printing on Normal 70 GSM Copier Paper:** While legally valid for basic xerox submissions, fragile, low-grade paper easily smudges when handled in humid exam rooms. Always use at least 180 GSM photo paper or get it laminated.
4. **Cropping Out the Date of Birth (DOB) or Gender:** Ensure the bottom demographic strip containing your full Date of Birth (`DD/MM/YYYY`) and Gender is fully captured. An Aadhaar showing only Year of Birth (`YYYY`) is rejected by UPSC and Passport Seva Kendras.
5. **Over-Laminating with Cheap Pouch Film:** Low-quality lamination pouches create air bubbles over the barcode. Always ensure the thermal laminator is pre-heated to 110°C–120°C for crystal-clear bonding.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the exact print size of an Aadhaar card in cm and mm?
The standard print size of an Aadhaar card is **85.60 mm in width by 53.98 mm in height** (or **8.56 cm × 5.40 cm**). This follows the international ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 credit-card standard.

### What is the default password to open an e-Aadhaar PDF file?
The password to open an e-Aadhaar PDF file is an 8-character combination: the **first 4 letters of your name in CAPITAL letters**, followed immediately by your **4-digit birth year** (for example, `ANIK1999`).

### Can I print my e-Aadhaar on normal A4 paper and laminate it?
