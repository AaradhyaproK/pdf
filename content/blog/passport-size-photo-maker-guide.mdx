## The Biometric "Golden Rules" for 100% Approval

Before editing, follow these five essential rules when snapping your raw portrait photo:

1. **Camera Position at Eye Level:** Have a friend hold the camera (or mount your phone on a tripod) approximately 1.5 to 2 meters (5 to 6 feet) away at direct eye level. Never use wide-angle selfie mode from an arm's reach—it distorts facial features by enlarging the nose and shrinking the ears.
2. **Neutral Lighting Without Hard Shadows:** Stand facing a large window during daylight hours. Diffused, indirect sunlight evenly illuminates both sides of your face and eliminates harsh shadows behind your ears and under your chin.
3. **Neutral Facial Expression:** Keep both eyes fully open and mouth gently closed. Smiling with visible teeth is an automatic reason for rejection by biometric immigration scanners.
4. **Eyeglasses and Headwear Guidelines:** Eyeglasses are strictly prohibited for US Visa photos and Indian passport submissions unless medically required with a signed doctor's note. Head coverings for religious reasons are permitted as long as the full oval of the face (from bottom of chin to top of forehead) is completely visible.
5. **Clothing Contrast:** Wear dark or colored clothing (navy blue, charcoal, burgundy) so your shoulders contrast sharply with the white or light-grey background.

### What is the difference between US Visa photo and Indian Passport photo?
The US Visa photo is a square format measuring **2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm or 600x600 pixels)** on a pure white background with 50-69% face coverage. The Indian Passport photo is rectangular measuring **3.5 x 4.5 cm (35 x 45 mm or 413x531 pixels)** with 70-80% face coverage on a white or light-colored background.

### Why do embassies reject passport photos?
The top 4 reasons for photo rejection are:
1. Uneven shadows across the face or background.
2. Tilting the head or looking away from the camera lens.
3. Glare or flash reflections on eyeglasses.
4. Over-compressing the image into blurry, pixelated artifacts.
