## 🏛️ What is UPSC? Full Form, Role & Overview

For beginners and aspirants starting their civil services preparation:

* **UPSC Full Form:** **Union Public Service Commission** (संघ लोक सेवा आयोग).
* **Constitutional Mandate:** Established under **Part XIV, Article 315 to 323** of the Constitution of India as the premier independent recruiting agency for Central and All India Services.
* **Headquarters:** Dholpur House, Shahjahan Road, New Delhi – 110069.
* **Major Examinations Conducted:** Civil Services Examination (CSE), National Defence Academy (NDA), Combined Defence Services (CDS), Indian Forest Service (IFoS), Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF AC), Combined Medical Services (CMS), and Engineering Services Examination (ESE).

## 📜 How to Check Previous Cycles: UPSC Result 2025 Archive

Candidates tracking merit lists, previous year cut-off scores, or roll-number PDF announcements from the previous recruitment cycle can access official commission archives directly:

1. Visit the official commission portal at **[`upsc.gov.in`](https://upsc.gov.in)**.
2. In the top navigation menu, click on **Examination** → **Active Examinations** or **Written Results**.
3. Under the **Civil Services Examination** section, click on the **UPSC Result 2025 (Final / Written / Cutoff)** download link.
4. Open the official PDF file and use `Ctrl + F` (or `Cmd + F` on Mac) to search for your 7-digit roll number or candidate name.

## 💻 Step-by-Step Guide: One-Time Registration (OTR) on upsconline.nic.in

The commission has made **One-Time Registration (OTR)** mandatory for all exams. You only need to register your basic profile once; your OTR ID remains valid for life.

### Step 1: Create Your OTR Profile
Visit **[`upsconline.nic.in`](https://upsconline.nic.in)** and click on **New Registration**. Fill in your legal name as printed on your Class 10th (Matriculation) certificate, father's name, mother's name, date of birth, mobile number, and active email address.

### Step 2: Set Security Questions & Password
Complete OTP authentication sent to your mobile and email. Save your permanent **14-digit OTR ID** in a secure location.

### Step 3: Fill the Civil Services (Prelims) Application
Log in using your registered OTR ID or Mobile Number. Select **Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination 2026** and choose your preferred examination centre.

### Step 4: Upload Mandatory Photograph, Signature & ID Proof
This is the single most critical step where thousands of application forms face rejection. Ensure your files adhere to the strict technical parameters detailed below.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the full form of UPSC?
The full form of UPSC is the **Union Public Service Commission**. It is the constitutional recruitment authority of India established under Article 315.

### What is the official UPSC Prelims 2026 exam date?
As per the tentative examination schedule announced by the commission, the UPSC Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination 2026 is scheduled to be held on **Sunday in May 2026**.

### Where can I check the UPSC Result 2025?
Candidates can download the official UPSC Result 2025 PDF merit list directly from the examination results archive on the official website at **`upsc.gov.in/examinations/examination-results`**.

### What are the photo dimensions for UPSC OTR registration?
UPSC requires a square aspect ratio photograph with minimum pixel dimensions of **350 × 350 pixels** and maximum of **1000 × 1000 pixels**, with a file size strictly between **20 KB and 300 KB** in JPG/JPEG format.

### Why do government portals reject photos with spectacles?
Even transparent prescription glasses reflect camera flash, causing lens glare that obscures the eyes and iris patterns. The automated facial recognition software used by UPSC flags this as unreadable, leading to application rejection.
