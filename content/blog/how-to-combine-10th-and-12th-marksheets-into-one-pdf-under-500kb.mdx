## 2. The 3 Costly Mistakes That Cause Application Rejection

Scrutiny officers flag and reject thousands of applications during certificate verification due to simple scanning oversights:

1. **Blurry Roll Numbers & Board Seals**: Over-compressing a file using low-quality cloud converters can smudge registration numbers, subjects, or school principal signatures, rendering the marksheet unreadable.
2. **Upside-Down or Sideways Pages**: If your Class 10 certificate is in portrait orientation while your Class 12 marksheet is upside down or landscape, verification software may reject the document.
3. **Password-Protected DigiLocker Downloads**: Official PDFs downloaded from DigiLocker or CBSE portals sometimes carry permission flags or password restrictions that prevent exam servers from reading the file contents. Always unlock or re-flatten the file prior to combining.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What order should marksheets be in when combining into one PDF?
Standard practice for almost all Indian competitive examinations and college admission forms is chronological: **Class 10 Marksheet on Page 1** (as it serves as primary legal verification for Date of Birth), followed by **Class 12 Marksheet on Page 2**. If you have separate passing certificates, place the 10th marksheet on Page 1, 10th certificate on Page 2, 12th marksheet on Page 3, and 12th certificate on Page 4.

### How do I ensure my combined PDF is below 500KB without becoming blurry?
The key is to avoid extreme JPEG compression on pre-rendered documents. Scanning at 150 to 200 DPI produces an ideal balance between file weight and sharpness. ### What if my marksheet is downloaded from DigiLocker?
DigiLocker marksheets are legally valid under Rule 9A of the Information Technology (Preservation and Retention of Information by Intermediaries Providing Digital Locker Facilities) Rules, 2016.
