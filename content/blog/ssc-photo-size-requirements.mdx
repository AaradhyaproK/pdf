The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts major national examinations across India, including **SSC CGL**, **CHSL**, **MTS**, **GD Constable**, **Stenographer**, and **CPO**.

Every examination cycle, tens of thousands of candidatures are rejected during scrutiny due to discrepancies in uploaded passport photographs and signatures.

Here are the complete official specifications, dimensions, file size windows, and verification criteria for SSC online application forms.

---

> **Official Portal Verification Notice:** Requirements may be updated periodically by exam authorities. Always verify notification specifics on the official SSC portal (`ssc.gov.in`) before submitting your final application.

---

## 1. SSC Photograph Official Specifications

- **File Weight:** **20 KB to 50 KB** (Files below 20KB or above 50KB cannot be uploaded).
- **Physical Dimensions:** **3.5 cm width × 4.5 cm height** (Aspect ratio 7:9).
- **Digital Pixel Resolution:** Approximately **413 × 531 pixels** (at 300 DPI) or 200 × 230 pixels minimum.
- **Background:** Plain white or light off-white background without shadows or patterns.
- **Pose & Facial Visibility:** Straight frontal view, with both eyes and both ears clearly visible. Neutral facial expression without tilted head.
- **Accessories:** Spectacles, sunglasses, caps, hats, or masks are strictly prohibited. Even clear prescription glasses should be removed to prevent flash reflections.

---

## 2. SSC Signature Official Specifications

- **File Weight:** **10 KB to 20 KB**.
- **Physical Dimensions:** **4.0 cm width × 2.0 cm height** (Aspect ratio 2:1).
- **Digital Pixel Resolution:** Approximately **400 × 200 pixels** (or 140 × 60 pixels).
- **Paper & Ink:** Signed on smooth, unruled white paper with black or dark blue ink.
- **Style:** Running handwriting only. Signatures in ALL CAPITAL (BLOCK) LETTERS are invalid and cause automatic rejection.

## Candidate Name & Date of Photo (DOP) Rules

In recent examination notifications, SSC has introduced live webcam photo capture alongside uploaded portraits. When an uploaded photo is requested:
- The photograph must have been taken within the last 3 months.
- Certain notifications request the candidate's name and Date of Photograph (DOP) printed at the bottom.

## Common Mistakes That Lead to SSC Rejection

1. **Uploading Blurred or Pixelated Photos:** Caused by taking screenshots of older physical photos.
2. **Wearing Caps or Dark Glasses:** Automatic rejection by automated image auditing software.
3. **Signatures in Capital Letters:** SSC guidelines explicitly require running cursive signatures.
4. **File Weight Under 20KB:** Attempting to over-compress below the 20KB minimum floor.
