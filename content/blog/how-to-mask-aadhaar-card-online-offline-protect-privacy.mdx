## 1. What Is a Masked Aadhaar Card?

A Masked Aadhaar card replaces the **first 8 digits** of your 12-digit Aadhaar number with cross characters (`XXXX-XXXX`), leaving only the **last 4 digits visible** (for example: `XXXX-XXXX-8921`).

### What Remains Visible on a Compliant Masked Aadhaar?
- Your legal full name.
- Your photograph.
- Your date of birth (or year of birth) and gender.
- Your permanent residential address.
- The digitally signed UIDAI verification QR code.
- The **last 4 digits** of your Aadhaar number.

This configuration fulfills all legitimate identity and address verification requirements while guaranteeing that unauthorized third parties cannot use your 12-digit biometric identifier to harvest personal data.

## 2. Official UIDAI & RBI Rules on Aadhaar Photocopies

Under **Section 29(4) of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016**, publishing or displaying an individual's full core biometric information or Aadhaar number without statutory authorization is strictly prohibited.

Furthermore:
- **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Direction on KYC (Section 16)**: Regulated financial institutions (banks, NBFCs, fintech apps) are instructed that if a customer provides an Aadhaar card for KYC offline, the first 8 digits must be masked or redacted.
- **Hotels, Guest Houses & Security Checkpoints**: UIDAI has clarified that private commercial establishments do not hold an Aadhaar authentication license and have **no legal authority** to collect or store unmasked 12-digit Aadhaar numbers. Providing a Masked Aadhaar card is 100% legally sufficient.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is a Masked Aadhaar card legally valid everywhere in India?
Yes. Under UIDAI notifications and the Aadhaar Act, 2016, a Masked Aadhaar card carries the exact same legal validity as a regular Aadhaar card for offline identity and address verification. Government departments, banks, telecom operators, hotels, airports, and private employers are legally mandated to accept Masked Aadhaar.

### Can hotels refuse check-in if I provide a Masked Aadhaar card?
No. UIDAI has explicitly clarified that hotels and commercial establishments have no legal entitlement to collect full 12-digit Aadhaar numbers. A Masked Aadhaar displaying your name, photograph, address, and the last 4 digits satisfies all requirements under the Innkeeper and Local Police verification guidelines.

### How do officials verify a Masked Aadhaar if 8 digits are hidden?
Any verifier can scan the printed secure QR code on the Masked Aadhaar using the official **mAadhaar app** or **UIDAI QR Code Reader**. The QR code is digitally signed with UIDAI’s cryptographic certificate and instantly reveals the holder's photograph, legal name, and demographic details without needing to expose the complete 12-digit number.

### What is the password to open my downloaded e-Aadhaar PDF?
