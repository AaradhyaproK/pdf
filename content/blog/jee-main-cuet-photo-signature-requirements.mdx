## The Critical "80% Face Coverage" Rule Explained

NTA notifications specifically emphasize:
> *"The recent photograph should be either in colour or black and white with 80% face (without mask) visible including ears against a white background."*

### What Does 80% Face Coverage Mean?
In casual smartphone portraits or selfie photos, your face often accounts for only 40% to 50% of the vertical frame, with the rest occupied by shoulders, chest, and ceiling or outdoor background.

* **Top of Frame:** There should be only a small margin (around 10%) above the crown of your head.
* **Bottom of Frame:** The crop should stop just below your collarbone or chin.
* **Ears Visible:** Your hair must be tucked behind your ears so that both ears and the full jawline are clearly visible.
* **Spectacles Note:** If you wear prescription spectacles daily, you may wear them, provided there is **zero glare or flash reflection** on the lenses and your pupils are completely unobstructed. Tinted glasses or polarized lenses are strictly forbidden.

## Name and Date of Photograph (DOP) Stamp Requirement

While NTA accepts plain photos with a white background, it strongly advises printing the candidate's name and the date of the photoshoot at the bottom of the photo to avoid discrepancy flags during counseling and seat allotment (JoSAA / CSAB):

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                              │
│                                              │
│              [ CANDIDATE FACE ]              │
│               (80% OCCUPANCY)                │
│                                              │
│                                              │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│               AARAV SHARMA                   │
│             D.O.P: 15/10/2026                │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Candidate Name:** Exactly matches the name on your Class 10 Board Marksheet.
2. **Date of Photo (DOP):** Must be a recent date within the last 3 months. Do NOT put your Date of Birth (DOB) by mistake!

## Top 5 Mistakes That Cause NTA Application Rejections

1. **Signing in CAPITAL / BLOCK Letters:** Signatures written in ALL CAPITAL letters are invalid under NTA rules. Use natural running handwriting.
2. **Blue Ink Pen:** NTA mandates signing with a **black ballpoint pen** on crisp white paper. Blue ink signatures can fail contrast thresholds during high-speed OMR scanning.
3. **Colored or Textured Backgrounds:** Selfies taken against bedroom walls, curtains, or outdoor gardens get instantly flagged. Only clean, pure white backgrounds are permitted.
4. **Heavy Shadows on Face:** Indoor lighting without a front fill-flash casts deep shadows under the eyes or chin. Ensure even, diffused lighting across both sides of the face.
5. **Blurry Certificate PDFs:** Uploading Category (OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, ST) or PwD certificates exceeding 300 KB or compressed to illegible low resolutions can result in category cancellation, forcing you into the General (Unreserved) quota. ## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the exact size required for JEE Main photograph?
For JEE Main 2026-2027, the passport photograph must be between **10 KB and 200 KB** in JPG/JPEG format with a white background and 80% face coverage.

### Is the Date of Photo (DOP) mandatory for JEE Main and CUET?
While NTA accepts clear photographs without text, having your Name and Date of Photograph printed at the bottom is strongly recommended to prevent identity verification flags during JoSAA counseling.

### What is the file size limit for JEE Main signature?
The digital signature for JEE Main must be between **4 KB and 30 KB** in JPG/JPEG format, signed with a black ballpoint pen on white paper.

### Can I wear glasses or spectacles in the JEE Main photo?
Yes, normal clear prescription spectacles are allowed if worn regularly, provided there is zero glare or flash reflection on the glass. Sunglasses and tinted lenses are strictly forbidden.

### What is the Postcard size photograph required for NTA exams?
The postcard photograph is a larger 4 inch by 6 inch (10 cm x 15 cm) print of the same photograph uploaded in your application form. It is required to be pasted on the designated attendance sheet in the examination hall.
