The portal requires an exact 1:1 crop to prevent facial distortion on the physical laminated card.

## Common Rejection Traps on NSDL and UTIITSL Portals

### 1. Uploading Standard 3.5 × 4.5 cm Photos Without Cropping
Most candidates try uploading their standard passport photo (aspect ratio 7:9). The PAN upload engine automatically squishes the rectangle into a 213×213 square, resulting in an artificially flattened or stretched face that fails biometric inspection. Always crop your photo to a **1:1 square** before uploading.

### 2. Using Low-Resolution 72 DPI Mobile Photos
Smartphone cameras shoot in 72 DPI screen resolution by default. While the image looks sharp on a phone screen, the PAN printing press requires **300 DPI metadata** to print fine micro-security patterns. If the metadata lacks 300 DPI tags, UTIITSL returns *"DPI check failed"*.

### 3. Signing with Blue or Gel Ink Pens
While NSDL technically permits dark blue ink, UTIITSL optical scanners frequently fail to detect blue ink strokes against white paper. **Always sign using a dark black ballpoint or fiber-tip pen** on crisp, unlined 75 GSM printer paper.

### 4. Shadowed or Yellowish Backgrounds
Photos captured against textured bedroom walls, wooden doors, or under yellow incandescent bulbs will be rejected. The background must be pure white or light grey with uniform lighting across both cheeks.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — PAN Card Photo & Signature

The photo must be a recent color passport photograph cropped to **213 × 213 pixels** (square 1:1 aspect ratio) at **300 DPI resolution**, with a file size strictly between **20 KB and 50 KB** in JPEG/JPG format.
