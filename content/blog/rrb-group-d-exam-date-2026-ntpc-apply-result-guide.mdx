## 💻 How to Apply on Official RRB Portal: rrbapply.gov.in

All Indian Railways recruitment applications are processed through the centralized **rrb apply** web engine. Candidates no longer need to submit paper forms or visit separate third-party websites.

### Step-by-Step RRB Apply Workflow:
1. **Portal Access:** Navigate to the authentic centralized recruitment website at [https://www.rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in).
2. **Account Creation:** Click on *"Apply"* followed by *"Create an Account"*. Enter your Aadhaar verification number, full name, date of birth, mobile number, and active email address.
3. **Application Form Filling:** Fill in your educational qualifications (10th/Matriculation, ITI, Diploma, or Degree), community category (UR, EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, ST), and preferred Railway Zone / Post priority.
4. **Biometric Uploads (Crucial Step):**
   - **Passport Photo:** Strictly between **30 KB and 70 KB** (JPG format, 35mm x 45mm, clear white background, no spectacles).
   - **Candidate Signature:** Strictly between **15 KB and 30 KB** (JPG format, running cursive hand with black or blue ink, no block capitals).
   - **SC/ST Travel Pass Scan (if applicable):** PDF document under **500 KB** for free sleeper class train authorization.
5. **Fee Payment & Confirmation:** Submit the application fee via net banking, UPI, or debit card. Always download and store a copy of your finalized application receipt and Registration ID.

## 📊 How to Check RRB Group D Result 2026 & Normalized Scorecard

Once the Computer Based Test (CBT) rounds conclude, the Railway Recruitment Board conducts an automated normalization process to ensure fairness across varying difficulty levels of multi-shift exams.

Here is the exact procedure to verify your **RRB Group D result 2026**:

1. **Visit Your Regional RRB Portal:** Go to the official website of the RRB zone you selected during registration (e.g., [RRB Chennai](https://www.rrbchennai.gov.in) or [RRB Kolkata](https://www.rrbkolkata.gov.in)).
2. **Locate the CEN Notification Link:** Click on the active link titled *"CEN Level-1 / Group D CBT Result & List of Shortlisted Candidates for PET"*.
3. **Download Result Merit List PDF:** The official result PDF contains the roll numbers of all candidates shortlisted for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET). Use `Ctrl + F` or `Cmd + F` to search for your roll number.
4. **Access the Candidate Scorecard:** Click the candidate login link to view your **raw score, pro-rata score, and normalized final mark**. Keep your Registration Number and Date of Birth handy.
5. **Prepare for PET (Physical Efficiency Test):** Qualified candidates must undergo physical fitness evaluations (carrying weights over a specified distance without putting it down, followed by timed running laps).

## ⚡ Photo & Signature Upload Rules: Avoid Instant Rejection

Every year, over 3 to 5 lakh railway applications face rejection due to formatting errors on the photo and signature upload stage. Keep these official parameters in mind:

### Railway Photo Specifications:
- **File Weight:** Must be strictly **30 KB to 70 KB**. (Photos below 30KB will trigger an error on the Railway portal).
- **Dimensions:** 35 mm x 45 mm (3.5 cm x 4.5 cm) or approximately 320 x 240 pixels.
- **Background:** Crisp, plain **white background**.
- **Important Restrictions:** No caps, no tinted glasses or spectacles, full face occupying 75-80% of the frame, both ears clearly visible.

### Railway Signature Specifications:
- **File Weight:** Must be strictly **15 KB to 30 KB**.
- **Dimensions:** 50 mm x 20 mm or 140 x 60 pixels.
- **Ink:** Clear black or blue ink on smooth white paper.
- **Style:** Running cursive handwriting. **BLOCK CAPITAL LETTERS ARE STRICTLY REJECTED.**

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the expected RRB Group D exam date 2026?
The RRB Group D (Level-1 Posts) examination stages are announced via official Centralized Employment Notices (CEN) published on the official Railway Recruitment portal `rrbapply.gov.in` and regional RRB websites. Because examinations take place across hundreds of test centers nationwide, the CBT stages span multiple weeks. Candidates should regularly monitor their regional RRB portals for official date sheets and city intimation slips.

### How can candidates complete their application on the rrb apply portal?
Candidates can register by visiting the official centralized portal at `https://www.rrbapply.gov.in`, creating a permanent user profile with Aadhaar verification, selecting their target Railway Zone and post preferences, uploading a compliant passport photograph (30KB to 70KB) and signature (15KB to 30KB), and completing online fee payment.

### Where can I check the RRB Group D result 2026 and cutoff marks?
The RRB Group D result 2026, category-wise cutoff marks, and candidate scorecards are released simultaneously on the 21 regional RRB websites (such as `rrbchennai.gov.in`, `rrbkolkata.gov.in`, and `rrbald.gov.in`). Candidates can log in with their Registration Number and Date of Birth to view their raw marks, normalized scores, and PET qualifying status.

### Why does the RRB portal reject photos under 30KB?
Unlike many public exam portals that accept photos down to 20KB, Indian Railways enforces a strict lower threshold of 30KB (and an upper limit of 70KB) to ensure high image clarity for thermal admit card printing and biometric facial verification at examination gates. Files smaller than 30KB or larger than 70KB are automatically blocked by the server upload script.

### What are the official links for RRB Chennai and RRB Kolkata?
The official web portal for Railway Recruitment Board Chennai is `https://www.rrbchennai.gov.in` and for Railway Recruitment Board Kolkata is `https://www.rrbkolkata.gov.in`. Always verify the `.gov.in` domain suffix to protect yourself from unofficial phishing and clone websites.
