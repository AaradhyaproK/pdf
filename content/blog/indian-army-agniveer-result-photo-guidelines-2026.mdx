## 🎖️ How to Check Indian Army Agniveer Result 2026 on joinindianarmy.nic.in

The **Common Entrance Examination (CEE)** results and merit lists are published region-wise by Army Recruiting Offices (AROs) and Zonal Recruiting Offices (ZROs).

### Step-by-Step Procedure to Download CEE Scorecard:
1. **Visit Official Defense Recruitment Portal:** Navigate to the authentic Indian Army website: 👉 [https://joinindianarmy.nic.in](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in).
2. **Solve Captcha Security Verification:** Enter the security code displayed on the screen to enter the main dashboard.
3. **Navigate to Agnipath Section:** On the homepage menu, click on the **"Agnipath"** tab or locate the **"CEE Results"** marquee.
4. **Select Your ARO / ZRO Notification:** Look for the notification header corresponding to your recruiting center (e.g., ARO Pune, ARO Ambala, ARO Danapur, ARO Lucknow, ARO Jaipur, ARO Bangalore, etc.).
5. **Download the Official Selection PDF:** The PDF contains the roll numbers of all candidates who cleared the CEE benchmark and qualified for the **Physical Fitness Test (PFT)** and **Medical Examination**.
6. **Candidate Login for Individual Marks:** Click on *"Agnipath Login"* and enter your registered Username (Email ID) and Password to view your individual CEE score.

## ✂️ Official "Army Cutting" (Haircut) & Biometric Photo Specifications

One of the most frequent reasons candidates face reprimand or disqualification at military rally screening centers is failure to adhere to armed forces grooming protocol, colloquially referred to as **"army cutting"** or the military crew cut.

### 1. Military Grooming & "Army Cutting" Rules:
- **Clean Military Haircut:** Hair must be neatly cropped on top with a tapered fade or short clipper trim on the sides and back (military buzz cut or high-and-tight standard). Long hair, uncombed locks, spiky styled hair, or dyed streaks are strictly forbidden.
- **Clean Shaven Requirement:** All non-Sikh candidates must report for documentation, photo capture, and physical rally grounds **completely clean-shaven**. Beards, goatees, and designer stubble are grounds for disqualification.
- **Sikh Candidates:** Sikh candidates wearing turbans must have their hair neatly tied and conform to traditional military presentation.

### 2. Passport Photograph Specifications for Rally & Portal:
| Parameter | Prescribed Military Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Dimensions** | **3.5 cm x 4.5 cm** (Standard Passport Size) |
| **File Weight (Online Portal)** | Strictly **20 KB to 50 KB** (JPG / JPEG format) |
| **Background Color** | Plain, unblemished **white background** |
| **Headgear & Eyewear** | No caps, no sunglasses, no dark spectacles |
| **Facial Positioning** | Full frontal portrait, neutral expression, both ears visible |
| **Physical Copies Needed at Rally** | At least **20 passport photographs** printed on good quality photographic paper |

## 🪖 Agniveer Army vs. Territorial Army (TA): Key Differences

Many aspirants explore opportunities across both the regular Agnipath pipeline and the **Territorial Army (TA)**:

| Feature | Agniveer Army | Territorial Army (TA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Age Criteria** | **17.5 to 21 years** | **18 to 42 years** |
| **Service Period** | 4-Year Engagement (with 25% retained into regular cadre) | Part-time citizen army service (2 months annual camp) |
| **Primary Eligibility** | 10th / 12th Pass depending on cadre (GD, Tech, Clerk) | Gainfully employed graduates / civilians |
| **Selection Mode** | Online CEE + Physical Rally + Medical | Written Exam (PIB) + SSB Interview + Medical |
| **Official Portal** | [joinindianarmy.nic.in](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in) | [jointerritorialarmy.gov.in](https://www.jointerritorialarmy.gov.in) |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Where is the official Indian Army Agniveer Result 2026 published?
The official Agniveer CEE examination results, merit lists, and recruitment rally notifications are published exclusively on the Indian Army's official recruitment portal at `https://joinindianarmy.nic.in` under the Agnipath section.

### What is the official "army cutting" haircut rule for Agniveer candidates?
Candidates appearing for Indian Army documentation, physical fitness rallies, and medical exams must have a standard short military crew cut (neatly tapered sides and short top). Non-Sikh candidates must also be completely clean-shaven without stubble or designer facial hair.

### What are the photo size limits on joinindianarmy.nic.in?
The online registration portal requires applicant passport photographs to be between **20 KB and 50 KB** in JPG/JPEG format, with dimensions of 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm and a plain white background. Signatures must be between **10 KB and 20 KB**.

### Can Territorial Army (TA) candidates also apply through joinindianarmy.nic.in?
Preliminary recruitment notifications for the Territorial Army for civilians and ex-servicemen are announced through `jointerritorialarmy.gov.in` and regional TA group headquarters, while general defense career resources are maintained across Indian Army websites.
