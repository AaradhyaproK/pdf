## Mastering the New SSC Live Photo Capture on `ssc.gov.in`

A major innovation of the new SSC portal is the integration of real-time browser camera verification. When filling out the OTR or exam application, candidates must take a live photograph using their computer webcam or smartphone camera via the SSC application app.

Thousands of aspirants struggle with recurrent errors like *"Face not detected"*, *"Improper lighting"*, or *"Background not plain"*. Follow these proven rules to clear verification on your first attempt:

### 1. Position Directly in Front of a Plain White Wall
The algorithmic facial recognition scanner requires high contrast around your silhouette. Stand 3 to 4 feet away from a clean white or light-cream wall. If you stand against a printed curtain, door frame, or cluttered bedroom, the system will reject the capture.

### 2. Leverage Diffuse Natural Window Lighting
Avoid harsh ceiling tubelights directly overhead, which cast dark shadows under your eyes and nose. Position yourself facing a window during daytime so soft natural light illuminates both sides of your face evenly.

### 3. Keep Eyes Level with the Camera Lens
Do not look down at your phone screen or tilt your chin upward. Hold the camera at eye level, look directly into the camera lens, keep your mouth closed with a neutral expression, and ensure both ears are clearly visible.

### 4. Remove Eyeglasses, Caps, and Headphones
Even if you wear prescription spectacles daily, remove them for the live capture. Camera flash and ambient lighting cause glass reflections that block the iris scanner.

A distinctive directive of the Staff Selection Commission is the requirement that uploaded passport photos must feature the **Candidate’s Full Name** and the **Date of Photograph (DOP)** printed clearly across the bottom section.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — SSC GD Constable & CGL

### 1. What are the official photo and signature dimensions for SSC GD Constable?
The photo must be a recent color passport photograph of **20 KB to 50 KB** with dimensions of **3.5 cm width by 4.5 cm height** (approx 100x120 pixels to 350x450 pixels) on a plain white background. The signature must be between **10 KB and 20 KB** with dimensions of **4.0 cm width by 2.0 cm height** (approx 140x60 pixels).

### 2. Is Date of Photo (DOP) compulsory on the SSC GD photograph?
Yes. Official Staff Selection Commission guidelines require that the photograph must not be older than three months from the notification date, and printing the Candidate Name and the Date of Photograph (DD/MM/YYYY) in a clean lower footer banner is strongly mandated to prevent disqualification.

### 3. How do I clear the Live Camera photo capture on the new ssc.gov.in portal?
Stand 3 to 4 feet in front of a well-lit, plain white wall during daytime. Look straight into the webcam lens with your eyes level, remove spectacles and caps, ensure both ears are visible, and hold still until the bounding box turns green and captures the frame.

### 4. Can I wear prescription eyeglasses in my SSC photo?
No. The commission explicitly states that spectacles, sunglasses, tinted glasses, or caps are strictly prohibited in both uploaded photographs and live webcam captures. Glare on lenses obscures pupil coordinates and causes automatic rejection.

### 5. Why does the portal say "Signature file size must be between 10 KB and 20 KB"?
If your signature file is even 1 byte below 10,240 bytes or 1 byte above 20,480 bytes, the upload server will reject it. Use FileZenith’s Signature Resizer, which locks the file safely between 14 KB and 18 KB.

### 6. Can I sign with a blue pen for SSC GD applications?
Both black and dark blue ink pens are acceptable on plain white unlined paper. However, black ink is strongly recommended because it produces higher contrast and scans with superior clarity through automated optical filters. Signatures in block capital letters will be rejected.

### 7. What happens if my SSC application is rejected due to a bad photo?
If your application is rejected at the scrutiny stage, you will not be issued an admit card for the Computer Based Examination (CBT). While SSC occasionally opens a brief 2-day "Application Form Correction Window", it requires an additional fee and is not guaranteed for all exams.
