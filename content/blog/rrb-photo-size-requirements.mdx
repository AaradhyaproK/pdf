The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB) conduct massive hiring drives across India for positions including **RRB NTPC (Graduate & Undergraduate)**, **Group D (Level 1)**, **ALP (Assistant Loco Pilot)**, and **Technician**.

The RRB application portal enforces strict lower limits on file size. Unlike many portals that accept photos down to 20KB, RRB specifically requires photos between **30KB and 50KB**. Photos under 30KB are automatically rejected by the portal's file pre-validator.

Here are the official specifications and instructions to prepare your railway recruitment photos without errors.

---

> **Official Portal Verification Notice:** Photo specifications are issued in official RRB Centralized Employment Notices (CEN). Always cross-reference your specific CEN on official regional RRB websites.

---

## 1. RRB Railway Photograph Specifications

- **File Size Window:** **30 KB to 50 KB** (Note the strict 30KB minimum threshold).
- **Physical Dimensions:** **35 mm × 45 mm** (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm).
- **Digital Resolution:** Minimum **320 × 240 pixels** up to **413 × 531 pixels**.
- **Background:** Clear, plain white or very light background.
- **Lighting & Features:** Full frontal portrait with both ears visible, neutral expression, and no shadows across face or background.
- **Prohibited Items:** Caps, dark sunglasses, masks, or headgear (except religious headwear where the full facial oval is unblocked).

---

## 2. RRB Railway Signature Specifications

- **File Size Window:** **10 KB to 20 KB**.
- **Dimensions:** Approximately **140 × 60 pixels** or 400 × 200 pixels.
- **Ink & Paper:** Black or dark blue ink on plain unruled white paper.
- **Format:** Cursive running hand. Block capital letters are strictly invalid.

---

## Why RRB Rejects Photos Below 30KB

Many candidates compress their photo down to 20KB or 25KB, only to receive the error *"File size must be between 30 KB and 50 KB"*.

The Railway portal's admit card printing pipeline utilizes thermal printers that require a higher minimum pixel density to prevent jagged pixelation on printed exam passes. Always target **38KB to 45KB** for RRB uploads.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Why does the RRB portal reject my photo under 30KB?
Railway Recruitment Board validation scripts explicitly require uploaded photos to weigh at least 30KB to guarantee clear, high-resolution printing on examination admit cards and attendance sheets.

### Can I wear glasses in my RRB photo?
Clear prescription spectacles are permitted, provided there are no flash glare or reflections obscuring the eyes. Tinted or colored glasses are strictly forbidden.

### What is the signature size required for RRB NTPC and Group D?
Signatures must weigh between **10KB and 20KB**, measuring roughly 140 × 60 pixels on a clean white background.
