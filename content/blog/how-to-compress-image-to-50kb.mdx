A **50KB maximum file ceiling** is the single most common requirement across major national examinations and recruitment portals in India, including **SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS)**, **IBPS PO & Clerk**, **SBI**, **Railway RRB**, and **State PSCs**.

These portals typically specify an exact upload window—most commonly **between 20KB and 50KB**. If your file is 19KB, it is rejected for low resolution; if it is 51KB, it is blocked for exceeding size limits.

This tutorial guides you through formatting your passport photo to land safely inside the 20KB–50KB bracket (optimally around 40KB–45KB) with crisp image quality.

---

## The 20KB to 50KB Portal Window Explained

Recruitment portals employ automated file filters during form submission:
- **Under 20KB:** Triggers "Image resolution too low" error.
- **Over 50KB:** Triggers "File size exceeds 50KB maximum" error.
- **Aspect Ratio Mismatch:** Distorts your face on the generated admit card.

Targeting an exact size of **40KB to 45KB** gives you the optimal safety margin, ensuring maximum visual fidelity while preventing any rejection.

---

## Recommended Passport Photo Dimensions

For most government recruitment portals:
- **Metric Size:** 3.5 cm width × 4.5 cm height
- **Digital Resolution (300 DPI):** 413 × 531 pixels
- **Banking Forms (IBPS/SBI):** 200 × 230 pixels (20KB–50KB)
- **Face Coverage:** 70% to 80% of frame, looking straight forward with both ears visible

## 3 Steps to Compress Passport Photo Under 50KB

### Step 1: Crop to Frontal Portrait Frame
Before compression, crop out excess background. Your head and upper shoulders should fill roughly 75% of the frame. Ensure the background is plain white or light off-white without harsh facial shadows.

### Step 2: Set Dimensions to 413 × 531 Pixels
Resize the canvas to standard 3.5 × 4.5 cm dimensions (413 × 531 px). Maintaining the 7:9 ratio prevents facial stretching or horizontal squashing.

### Step 3: Run Binary Search Compression
Using our client-side [Compress Image to 50KB Tool](/tools/compress-image-to-50kb), drag and drop your photo. The browser engine will iteratively calculate the quality factor (typically 0.80 to 0.88) to output a 42KB JPEG in under 50 milliseconds.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Uploading Non-White Backgrounds:** Many recruitment boards reject photos with colorful, dark, or outdoor backgrounds.
- **Overshooting Below 20KB:** Applying excessive compression reduces file weight below 20KB, triggering minimum resolution errors.
- **Wearing Hats or Dark Sunglasses:** Always submit clear photos with eyes open and unshaded.
