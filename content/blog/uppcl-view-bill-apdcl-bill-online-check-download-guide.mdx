## 🔌 UPPCL View Bill: Complete Guide for Uttar Pradesh Consumers

The **Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Limited (UPPCL)** distributes electricity across UP through five regional distribution companies (DISCOMs):
1. **PVVNL (Paschimanchal):** Meerut, Noida, Ghaziabad, Bulandshahr, Moradabad, Saharanpur.
2. **MVVNL (Madhyanchal):** Lucknow, Ayodhya, Bareilly, Badaun, Rae Bareli, Sitapur.
3. **DVVNL (Dakshinanchal):** Agra, Mathura, Aligarh, Kanpur Dehat, Jhansi, Banda.
4. **PuVVNL (Purvanchal):** Varanasi, Prayagraj, Gorakhpur, Azamgarh, Mirzapur.
5. **KESCO:** Dedicated electricity supply for Kanpur City urban areas.

### How to Find Your UPPCL Consumer Account Number
To perform a **uppcl view bill** lookup, you need your unique account number:
* **Urban Consumers:** Standard **10-digit Account ID** (found at the top right of any previous electricity bill).
* **Rural Consumers:** New **12-digit Account ID** (migrated by UPPCL to standardise rural revenue accounting).

---

### Step-by-Step: UPPCL View Bill Online (Without Login / Quick Pay)

You do not need to register a user account to view your bill summary or current payable amount:

1. Visit the official UPPCL Consumer Portal: 👉 [https://consumer.upegov.in](https://consumer.upegov.in) (or [upenergy.in](https://www.upenergy.in)).
2. Select your respective DISCOM from the dropdown menu (**Madhyanchal, Paschimanchal, Dakshinanchal, Purvanchal**, or **KESCO**).
3. Enter your **10-digit (Urban)** or **12-digit (Rural)** Account Number.
4. Enter the security Captcha code shown on the screen.
5. Click **"View / Submit"**.
6. Your bill details appear instantly:
   * **Consumer Name & Address**
   * **Bill Issue Date & Due Date**
   * **Units Consumed (kWh)**
   * **Current Cycle Energy Charges**
   * **Net Payable Amount (with early payment rebate and surcharge if unpaid)**

---

### How to Download UPPCL Bill Payment Receipt PDF

If you need your electricity bill as an official address proof or for income tax reimbursement:

1. After viewing your bill on `consumer.upegov.in`, click **"View Bill (PDF)"** or **"View Receipt"** if already paid.
2. The complete itemized invoice loads with the UPPCL official watermark and payment QR code.
3. Click the **Print** icon or press `Ctrl + P` / `Cmd + P`.
4. Select **"Save as PDF"** to store the document on your smartphone or PC.

## 💡 APDCL Bill: Complete Guide for Assam Electricity Consumers

The **Assam Power Distribution Company Limited (APDCL)** is the sole electricity distributor for the state of Assam, powering over 60 lakh domestic, commercial, and industrial consumers.

### What is the APDCL 12-Digit Consumer Number?
Every APDCL consumer connection is linked to a **12-digit Consumer Number** (e.g., `101000123456`). This number is printed clearly on your physical meter bill or meter card.

---

### Step-by-Step: APDCL Bill Online Check & Quick Pay

To inspect your **apdcl bill** online without an account:

1. Open the official APDCL website: 👉 [https://www.apdcl.org](https://www.apdcl.org).
2. On the homepage banner, click on **"Pay Electricity Bill"** or **"View Bill"**.
3. In the input box, enter your **12-Digit Consumer Number**.
4. Enter the Captcha verification code.
5. Click **"Verify"** / **"Process"**.
6. The dashboard displays your complete billing summary:
   * **Consumer Name & Sub-Division Office**
   * **Latest Meter Reading & Units Consumed**
   * **Bill Month & Bill Due Date**
   * **Current Energy Charges & FPPPA Surcharge**
   * **Net Total Payable Amount**

You can click **"Pay Now"** to settle the dues immediately via UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, or Net Banking.

---

### APDCL Smart Prepaid Meter: Balance Check & Recharge

Assam is rapidly transitioning to smart prepaid digital meters. If your home has an APDCL smart meter installed:

1. Download the **APDCL Smart Meter (EasyPay)** mobile app or visit `apdcl.org`.
2. Enter your **12-digit Consumer Number**.
3. View your **Current Account Balance (₹)**, daily unit consumption trends, and estimated days remaining before balance exhaustion.
4. If your balance dips below ₹100, recharge online instantly using UPI to prevent automatic power disconnection.

---

### How to Download APDCL Bill Payment Receipt PDF

1. After making an online payment on `apdcl.org`, click on **"Print Receipt"**.
2. If retrieving an earlier payment, navigate to the **"Receipt Print"** tab on the APDCL portal and enter your 12-digit Consumer Number.
3. The official e-receipt featuring the APDCL transaction ID, consumer details, and amount paid will open.
4. Save the document as a PDF on your device.

## 📊 Tariff Comparison: UPPCL vs APDCL Domestic Slabs (2026)

Understanding how your bill is calculated helps you detect billing errors. Both UP and Assam follow a **telescopic slab structure**:

| Consumption Tier | UPPCL Domestic (Urban) Rate | APDCL Domestic-A (Up to 5kW) Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0 to 100 / 120 Units** | **₹5.50 / unit** (0–100 units) | **₹5.40 / unit** (0–120 units, post-subsidy) |
| **101 to 150 / 240 Units** | **₹6.00 / unit** (101–150 units) | **₹6.65 / unit** (121–240 units) |
| **151 to 300 Units** | **₹6.50 / unit** (151–300 units) | **₹7.65 / unit** (Above 240 units) |
| **Above 300 Units** | **₹7.00 / unit** (Above 300 units) | **₹7.65 / unit** (Tier rate) |
| **Monthly Fixed Demand Charge** | **₹110 / kW / month** | **₹75 / kW / month** |
| **Electricity Duty / Tax** | **5%** | **5%** |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How can I check my UPPCL view bill without login?
Visit the official UPPCL portal at [consumer.upegov.in](https://consumer.upegov.in) or [upenergy.in](https://www.upenergy.in). Select your distribution discom (PVVNL, MVVNL, DVVNL, PuVVNL, or KESCO), enter your 10-digit (Urban) or 12-digit (Rural) account number, solve the captcha, and click View. Your total bill amount and due date appear immediately without requiring any password.

### What is the official website for APDCL bill payment and view?
The official website for Assam Power Distribution Company Limited is [https://www.apdcl.org](https://www.apdcl.org). You can view monthly bills, verify smart meter balances, and pay electricity dues directly with your 12-digit consumer number.

### How do I find my UPPCL account number if I only have my meter number?
If you have lost your UPPCL account number, check any previous SMS alert received on your registered mobile number from UPPCL, or call the 24x7 toll-free helpline at **1912** and provide your meter serial number to retrieve your 10-digit or 12-digit account ID.

### How can I check my APDCL smart prepaid meter balance?
You can check your APDCL smart prepaid meter balance on the APDCL EasyPay mobile app or by visiting [apdcl.org](https://www.apdcl.org). Enter your 12-digit consumer number to check current live balance, daily deduction, and recharge history.

### Can I download an electricity bill payment receipt for address proof?
Yes. Both UPPCL (`consumer.upegov.in`) and APDCL (`apdcl.org`) allow you to download official digital payment receipts and duplicate bill PDFs with authorized seals and QR codes. If the portal file is too large to upload for your passport or exam application, use FileZenith's [Compress PDF to 100KB](/pdf/compress#100kb) to compress it safely without cloud uploads.

### How is the electricity bill calculated from meter reading?
Take your current meter reading in kWh and subtract your previous month reading to determine net units consumed. Your discom charges these units across telescopic slab brackets (e.g., first 100 units at lower rates, higher units at elevated tiers), adds fixed demand charges per kW load, fuel surcharge (FPPPA), and electricity duty (tax).
