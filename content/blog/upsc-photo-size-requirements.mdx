The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) requires all aspirants registering on the One Time Registration (OTR) portal for examinations including **Civil Services (IAS, IFS, IPS)**, **CDS**, **NDA**, **CAPF**, and **IES** to upload photographs and signatures conforming to strict updated guidelines.

Unlike other recruitment boards that utilize rectangular 3.5 × 4.5 cm frames, UPSC mandates a **1:1 square aspect ratio** and requires the candidate's name and photo date clearly visible on the image.

---

> **Official Portal Verification Notice:** Requirements may change. Always verify the latest guidelines on the official UPSC portals (`upsc.gov.in` and `upsconline.nic.in`) before submitting your application.

---

## 1. UPSC Photograph Official Guidelines (OTR Portal)

- **Aspect Ratio:** **1:1 Perfect Square** (Non-square images are rejected by the OTR validator).
- **Pixel Dimensions:** Minimum **350 × 350 pixels**, Maximum **1000 × 1000 pixels**.
- **File Weight:** **20 KB to 300 KB**.
- **Recency:** Must have been photographed within **10 days** of the start of the online application process.
- **Name & Date Stamp:** The candidate's full name and the date on which the photo was captured must be clearly inscribed at the lower portion of the photograph.
- **Face Coverage:** Facial appearance must cover roughly **75%** of the photograph frame.

---

## 2. UPSC Signature Official Guidelines

- **Resolution:** Minimum **350 × 350 pixels**, Maximum **1000 × 1000 pixels** (Square aspect ratio).
- **File Weight:** **20 KB to 300 KB**.
- **Format:** JPEG format on a clean white sheet with black ink.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Uploading Rectangular Photos:** Attempting to submit standard 3.5 × 4.5 cm rectangular photos triggers an immediate aspect ratio mismatch error.
- **Outdated Photographs:** Submitting photos older than 10 days violates UPSC OTR exam instructions.
- **Low Pixel Resolution:** Ensuring both width and height are at least 350 pixels prevents upload failures.
