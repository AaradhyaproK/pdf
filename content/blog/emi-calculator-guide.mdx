Planning a loan for a new home, car, or personal financial need requires understanding how your Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is determined. Knowing the breakdown between principal repayment and interest charges helps you choose optimal loan tenures and save on interest.

---

## How EMI Is Calculated: Formula Explained

The standard mathematical formula for calculating EMI is:

`EMI = [P x r x (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n - 1]`

Where:
- **P** = Principal loan amount
- **r** = Monthly interest rate (Annual rate / 12 / 100)
- **n** = Loan tenure in total number of months

Understanding this interest amortization curve helps borrowers understand why early EMI installments go mostly toward interest payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does increasing loan tenure reduce overall loan cost?
No. While a longer tenure reduces your monthly EMI amount, it significantly increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

### Can pre-payments reduce total interest burden?
Yes! Making partial pre-payments early in your loan tenure directly reduces the outstanding principal, exponentially decreasing future interest accumulation.
