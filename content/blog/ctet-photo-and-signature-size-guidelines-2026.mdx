## 1. Official CBSE CTET Photo & Signature Specifications

The Central Board of Secondary Education publishes strict photographic criteria in the official CTET Information Bulletin. Both the photograph and signature scan must strictly comply with the following parameters:

### A. Candidate Photograph Requirements
- **File Format:** Strictly **JPG or JPEG** (PNG, PDF, and HEIC files are rejected by the CTET server).
- **File Size Limit:** **10 KB (minimum) to 100 KB (maximum)**. CBSE recommends an ideal compressed size of **50 KB to 70 KB** for high clarity.
- **Physical Dimensions:** **3.5 cm (width) × 4.5 cm (height)**.
- **Pixel Resolution:** Approximately **413 × 531 pixels** at standard 300 DPI web rasterization.
- **Background Color:** Clear, plain **white or light off-white background**. Colored walls, patterns, or outdoor backgrounds are strictly prohibited.
- **Face Coverage:** The applicant's face must occupy **70% to 80%** of the frame, showing both ears, eyes, and shoulders looking straight into the camera lens.
- **Eyewear & Accessories:** Caps, hats, tinted sunglasses, dark goggles, and heavy facial coverings are strictly disallowed. Regular prescription spectacles are permitted provided there is zero lens glare or flash reflection.

### B. Candidate Signature Requirements
- **File Format:** Strictly **JPG or JPEG**.
- **File Size Limit:** **3 KB (minimum) to 30 KB (maximum)**.
- **Physical Dimensions:** **3.5 cm (width) × 1.5 cm (height)**.
- **Pixel Resolution:** Approximately **413 × 177 pixels**.
- **Ink & Paper:** Must be signed with a **black or dark blue ballpoint pen** on clean white unruled paper. Fountain pens or gel pens that bleed into paper fiber should be avoided.
- **Handwriting Style:** The signature must be in **running cursive handwriting**. Signatures in **ALL CAPITAL BLOCK LETTERS or initials only are permanently rejected**.

## 3. Four Costly Mistakes That Cause CTET Application Rejection

Before submitting your CTET application form on `ctet.nic.in`, verify your files against these common errors:

1. **Signatures in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS:** CBSE examiners treat all-caps signatures as non-compliant. Your signature must mirror your natural running handwriting on bank and school documents.
2. **Shadows on White Walls:** Taking a photo against a white bedroom wall without proper lighting casts dark silhouettes behind your ears and shoulders. Use even daytime front lighting or remove the background with an [AI Background Remover](/image/remove-background).
3. **Over-Compressing Below 10 KB or 3 KB:** Many free online compression tools crush image files down to 2 KB or 5 KB, making text illegible and falling below CTET's 10 KB photo or 3 KB signature minimum threshold.
4. **Incorrect Aspect Ratio Distortion:** Squeezing a horizontal smartphone selfie into a 3.5×4.5 cm portrait box stretches the face unnaturally. Always crop the image before resizing.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the exact photo size required for CTET 2026?
CBSE CTET mandates that candidate photographs must be in JPG or JPEG format, between 10 KB and 100 KB in file size, with physical dimensions of 3.5 cm in width and 4.5 cm in height (approx. 413 × 531 pixels).

### What is the signature file size and dimension for CTET?
The candidate signature scan must be between 3 KB and 30 KB in file size, in JPG/JPEG format, with physical dimensions of 3.5 cm in width and 1.5 cm in height (approx. 413 × 177 pixels).

### Can I sign in capital letters for the CTET form?
No. CBSE strictly prohibits signatures written in all capital letters. Your signature must be in your natural running cursive handwriting. Signatures in capital letters will lead to application cancellation.

### Can I wear glasses or spectacles in my CTET photo?
Yes, regular prescription spectacles are allowed as long as your eyes are clearly visible and there is no camera flash reflection or glare on the lenses. Sunglasses, tinted glasses, or spectacles with thick decorative frames that obstruct the eyes are not allowed.
