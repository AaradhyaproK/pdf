Submitting photos for government recruitment examinations, national university admissions, or passport and visa renewals requires following precise dimensions, aspect ratios, and file size constraints.

Uploading a casual selfie, an uncropped snapshot, or an incorrectly sized image is the single most common cause of application rejection or verification delays.

This step-by-step tutorial teaches you how to prepare, crop, and format your passport photograph to meet strict portal standards without needing professional photo studio software.

---

## 4 Rules of an Acceptable Application Photo

1. **Frontal Eye-Level Pose:** Look straight into the camera lens with a neutral facial expression. Both eyes and both ears must be clearly visible.
2. **Face-to-Frame Coverage:** The applicant's face (chin to crown) and upper shoulders must occupy **70% to 80%** of the entire vertical frame.
3. **Background & Lighting:** Use a clean, plain white or very light off-white background. Avoid harsh single-side shadows across your face or neck.
4. **No Accessories:** Remove sunglasses, tinted eyeglasses, caps, and headwear (unless worn for religious requirements, where the entire facial oval must remain visible).

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Format Your Application Photo

### Step 1: Capture a Clean Portrait
Stand approximately 4 to 5 feet away from a plain white or light wall with soft natural daylight facing you. Ask a family member or friend to snap the photo at eye level—avoid handheld selfies as arm extension introduces wide-angle facial distortion.

### Step 2: Crop to Correct Aspect Ratio
Most portals mandate a **3.5 cm width × 4.5 cm height** (approx 7:9 aspect ratio) or **1:1 square** (e.g. UPSC OTR, US Visa). Use [FileZenith's Photo Resizer Studio](/tools/ssc-photo-resizer) to crop with guided overlay grids.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using Selfies:** Front cameras introduce fisheye lens distortion and show angled shoulders.
- **Tilting the Head:** Keep head vertical and centered.
- **Old Photographs:** Portals require photos taken within 10 days to 3 months of application submission.
- **Blurry Scans:** Always work with clear, high-resolution original shots before cropping.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I take my passport photo using a mobile smartphone?
Yes! A modern smartphone camera takes more than enough detail. Stand in front of a white wall with bright daylight facing you, capture a straight portrait, and use FileZenith to crop and resize to exact portal specifications.

### What is the standard passport photo dimension in pixels?
At standard 300 DPI print resolution, a 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm passport photo translates to **413 pixels wide by 531 pixels high**. For banking portals (IBPS/SBI), the standard specification is **200 × 230 pixels**.

### What should I do if my photo has a dark or cluttered background?
Crop the image tightly around your head and shoulders. If the background remains cluttered, retake the photo in front of a plain white wall or bedsheet to prevent portal rejection.
