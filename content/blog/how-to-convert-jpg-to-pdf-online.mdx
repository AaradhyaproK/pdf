## The Common Challenges of Converting Images to PDF

When combining several camera photos into a single PDF, most people run into three common technical pitfalls:

1. **Uncontrolled File Size Explosion:** Smartphone cameras today capture 12MP to 48MP images, averaging 4MB to 8MB per photograph. Combining 5 photos directly without downsampling produces a 30MB+ PDF file that crashes submission portals (which usually enforce strict 2MB or 500KB caps).
2. **Mismatched Page Orientations & Aspect Ratios:** Some certificate photos were taken in landscape mode while others are in portrait. Without an intelligent page layout engine, landscape certificates get cut in half or rotated 90 degrees sideways inside the PDF.
3. **Loss of Fine Text Clarity:** Basic tools aggressively compress images into low-bitrate JPEGs, turning fine numerical grades on mark sheets and legal clauses on contracts into unreadable blurs.

Using an intelligent client-side converter like [FileZenith Pics to PDF](/image/to-pdf) solves all three issues: images are auto-oriented, custom margins are applied, and vector font streams are preserved so that text stays razor-sharp while keeping total file size compact.

## 5 Pro Tips for Flawless Image-to-PDF Conversion

1. **Shoot in Bright, Even Natural Lighting:** Shadows cast by your phone or hand across a page degrade OCR (Optical Character Recognition) quality. Take document photos near a window or under direct daylight for uniform white paper backgrounds.
2. **Flatten Curved Pages:** If scanning bound books or multi-page brochures, press pages flat before photographing to avoid distorted curved lines that look unprofessional.
3. **Crop Unneeded Background Objects:** Crop out wooden desks, bedsheets, or keyboard edges using our [Image Cropper](/image/crop) before converting to PDF. Removing excess background pixels cuts final file size by up to 35%.
4. **Name Your Pages Sequentially:** Before uploading from your computer, renaming your files `01-degree.jpg`, `02-marksheet.jpg`, and `03-experience.jpg` ensures automatic alphabetical sorting in the upload queue.
5. **Compress Further If Still Over 200KB:** If your recruitment portal demands an ultra-strict file size ceiling (like UPSC 300KB or SSC 200KB), pass your generated document through our specialized [Compress PDF to 200KB](/pdf/compress-to-200kb) utility.
