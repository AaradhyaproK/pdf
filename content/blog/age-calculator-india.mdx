Many competitive exam application forms, government job portals (UPSC, SSC, Banking), and official registration documents require entering your exact age as of a specific cutoff date (e.g. "Age as on January 1st").

Calculating your age manually across leap years and varying month lengths can lead to errors.

---

## Why Exact Age Calculations Matter for Government Portals

Official application portals strictly enforce minimum and maximum age criteria down to the exact day:
- **Cutoff Dates**: Portals require age as of a specified reference date rather than the current day.
- **Leap Year Adjustments**: Manual counting often ignores 29-day February leap years.
- **Verification Records**: Discrepancies during document verification can lead to application disqualification.
