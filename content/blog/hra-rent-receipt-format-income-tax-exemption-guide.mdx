## How Is HRA Tax Exemption Calculated? (The 3-Part Rule)

Under **Rule 2A of the Income Tax Rules**, the exempt portion of your House Rent Allowance is strictly calculated as the **least (minimum)** of the following three values:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               HRA Tax Exemption Formula                     │
│  Exempt Amount = MINIMUM of the following 3 amounts:       │
│                                                             │
│  1. Actual HRA received from your employer                  │
│  2. Actual Rent Paid MINUS 10% of Basic Salary (+ DA)       │
│  3. 50% of Basic Salary (Metro) OR 40% (Non-Metro)          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Note on Metro Cities:** Metro cities for HRA calculations are strictly defined as **Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, and Chennai** (50% rule). High-growth tech hubs such as **Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurugram, and Noida** fall under the **40% (Non-Metro) category** for Income Tax calculation purposes.

### Practical Calculation Example:
* **Basic Salary:** ₹50,000/month (₹6,00,000/year)
* **Actual HRA Received:** ₹20,000/month (₹2,40,000/year)
* **Rent Paid (Mumbai Metro):** ₹22,000/month (₹2,64,000/year)

**Evaluating the 3 Conditions:**
1. **Actual HRA:** ₹2,40,000
2. **Rent Paid minus 10% of Basic:** ₹2,64,000 - ₹60,000 = **₹2,04,000**
3. **50% of Basic Salary (Metro):** 50% of ₹6,00,000 = **₹3,00,000**

**Result:** The lowest value is **₹2,04,000**.
* **Tax-Exempt HRA:** ₹2,04,000 (Saved from taxable income!)
* **Taxable HRA:** ₹2,40,000 - ₹2,04,000 = ₹36,000 (Added to taxable income).

## The ₹1 Revenue Stamp Rule: When Is It Legally Required?

One of the most frequent questions asked by tenants is whether a **₹1 red revenue stamp** is mandatory on rent receipts.

According to the **Indian Stamp Act, 1899**:
* **Cash Rent Payments over ₹5,000:** If you pay more than ₹5,000 in cash per receipt, the landlord must affix a **₹1 revenue stamp** and sign or initial *across* the face of the stamp.
* **Online & Banking Payments (NEFT, UPI, Cheque):** If you pay via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or direct net banking transfer, the electronic bank ledger serves as legal receipt of payment. In this case, while your HR will still ask for a signed rent receipt document for administrative tracking, **a physical revenue stamp is not strictly mandatory under the law**.
* **Best Corporate Practice:** If your monthly rent is above ₹10,000, affixing a ₹1 revenue stamp with your landlord’s signature across it completely eliminates any potential HR query or audit rejection.

## Mandatory Elements of a Valid HRA Rent Receipt

To ensure your rent receipt passes corporate HR and Income Tax scrutiny with zero hassle, it must feature these essential data points:

1. **Date of Payment & Tenancy Period:** Clearly state the rental month (e.g., *"Rent for the month of November 2026"*).
2. **Tenant & Landlord Full Legal Names:** Must match Aadhaar / PAN records.
3. **Full Address of the Rented Property:** Complete with house number, building name, locality, and PIN code.
4. **Exact Amount Paid:** Both in numerical figures (e.g. ₹18,500) and in words.
5. **Mode of Payment:** Cash, UPI transaction ID, Cheque number, or NEFT reference.
6. **Landlord’s PAN Number:** Compulsory if annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000.
7. **Landlord Signature:** Across revenue stamp (for cash > ₹5,000) or at the bottom signature line.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — HRA Rent Receipts & Tax Rules

### 1. Is landlord PAN card mandatory for claiming HRA?
Yes, if your annual rent exceeds **₹1,00,000** (approx. **₹8,334 per month**), citing your landlord's Permanent Account Number (PAN) is legally mandatory under Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) guidelines. If your landlord does not have a PAN, they must provide a signed declaration in **Form 60**.

### 2. Is revenue stamp required if I pay rent through Google Pay or UPI?
Legally, under the Indian Stamp Act, revenue stamps are specifically required for cash acknowledgments exceeding ₹5,000. For digital payments (UPI, NEFT, IMPS), bank records act as the electronic audit trail. However, many corporate HR portals still request a signed receipt with a revenue stamp to standardize documentation.

### 3. Can I pay rent to my parents and claim HRA?
Yes! You can legally pay rent to your parents and claim HRA exemption under Section 10(13A), provided your parents are the legal owners of the residential property. You must transfer the rent to their bank account and generate monthly receipts. Your parents must report this rental income in their personal Income Tax Returns (ITR).

### 4. Can I pay rent to my spouse and claim HRA?
No. Indian tax tribunals and courts do not permit HRA claims for rent paid to a spouse. The relationship of marriage inherently involves living together as a single household, and paying rent to a husband or wife is viewed as a tax avoidance sham.

### 5. Can I claim both HRA and Home Loan Tax Deductions simultaneously?
Yes. You can claim both HRA exemption (Section 10(13A)) and Home Loan interest deductions (Section 24(b) up to ₹2 Lakh) if you own a home in one city or locality but live in a rented accommodation in another city for employment reasons.
