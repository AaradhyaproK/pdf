## Why Do Government Portals Insist on a 100KB Limit?

You might wonder why modern government portals in 2026 still enforce such small file limits when high-speed broadband is everywhere:

1. **Massive Server Load:** When **30 to 40 lakh candidates** apply for exams like SSC or Railway NTPC simultaneously, storing 5MB files per applicant would require petabytes of high-availability cloud storage. A 100KB cap keeps database queries instantaneous and server infrastructure lean.
2. **Automated Document Screening:** Modern verification portals use automated Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to read candidate roll numbers, names, and passing dates directly from the uploaded PDF. A standardized, compressed PDF processes in milliseconds without crashing the server.

## 4 Pro Tips for Scanning Certificates Under 100KB

If you are scanning physical paper certificates with a smartphone scanner app (like Adobe Scan, Microsoft Lens, or CamScanner), keep these golden rules in mind:

1. **Use Grayscale or Document Mode:** Scanning in 24-bit True Color quadruples the raw file size. For black-and-white marks sheets and legal certificates, selecting **Document / Grayscale Mode** cuts baseline file size by 65% before compression.
2. **Crop Excess Background Margins:** Remove wooden table edges, bedsheets, or extra shadows around the paper certificate. Extraneous pixels add unnecessary kilobyte overhead.
3. **The 150 DPI Sweet Spot:** 300 DPI is only necessary for physical commercial printing. For digital screen verification and government uploads, **150 DPI** offers the perfect balance of pin-sharp text clarity and compact file size.
4. **Avoid Multiple Scans in One File:** Government portals usually require each document (Class 10 marksheet, degree certificate, caste certificate) as separate 1-page PDF files under 100KB. Ensure you only scan the single required page.
