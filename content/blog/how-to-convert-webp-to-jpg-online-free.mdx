## What is WebP and Why Does Software Reject It?

Developed by Google in 2010 based on VP8 video compression technology, **WebP** was engineered specifically to reduce internet bandwidth. Websites like YouTube, Reddit, eBay, and news portals serve WebP images because they are typically **25% to 34% smaller** than equivalent JPEGs while looking virtually identical.

However, WebP creates major compatibility headaches for desktop and offline workflows:

1. **Legacy Office Applications:** Older versions of Microsoft Word (Word 2016 and older), PowerPoint, and Excel cannot embed WebP images directly.
2. **Offline Photo Editors:** Standalone software like Photoshop CS6, CorelDRAW, and GIMP often require third-party plugins just to open a `.webp` container.
3. **Institutional & Government Portals:** Portals for exam applications (UPSC, SSC, Railway RRB, IBPS), passport portals, visa applications, and university admissions only accept `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, or `.png`. Uploading a `.webp` file causes immediate upload validation errors.
4. **Digital Picture Frames & TV Displays:** Most smart TVs, digital photo frames, and projector USB drives do not have built-in WebP decoders.

## Comparison: WebP vs. JPG vs. PNG

Understanding when to keep WebP and when to convert to JPG helps you manage your digital image collection efficiently:

| Feature / Metric | WebP (.webp) | JPEG / JPG (.jpg) | PNG (.png) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Use Case** | Modern website speed & web delivery | Universal offline sharing, forms & photography | Graphics with transparent backgrounds & logos |
| **Global Compatibility** | Modern browsers only (Chrome, Safari, Edge) | **100% universal across all devices & software** | Universal across all devices & software |
| **Transparency (Alpha)** | Supported | **Not supported (replaced with white background)**| Supported |
| **Typical File Size** | Smallest (up to 35% smaller than JPG) | Compact & adjustable compression | Larger (lossless vector & bitmap) |
| **Submission Portals** | Frequently rejected | **Universally accepted by all portals** | Accepted by most portals |

## How to Convert WebP to JPG on Windows & Mac Without Tools

If you are offline and need to convert a single file quickly, you can use built-in operating system utilities:

### Method 1: Using Windows Paint
1. Right-click the `.webp` image in Windows File Explorer.
2. Select **Open with > Paint**.
3. Click **File > Save as > JPEG picture**.
4. Choose your destination folder and click **Save**.

### Method 2: Using Mac Preview
1. Double-click the `.webp` image to open it in macOS **Preview**.
2. Click **File > Export...** in the top menu bar.
3. In the **Format** dropdown, select **JPEG**.
4. Adjust the quality slider and click **Save**.

*Note: While built-in desktop tools work for individual images, they do not support bulk or batch conversion. If you have 10, 20, or 50 images, using an in-browser batch converter like [FileZenith WebP to JPG](/image/webp-to-jpg) is dramatically faster.*
